About SalahTime

SalahTime provides accurate daily prayer times for 140 cities across the Muslim world — free, with no account and no signup. Prayer schedules are computed entirely in your browser using established astronomical formulas, so the site is fast, private, and keeps working offline once loaded.

We support eleven official calculation methods — including the Egyptian General Authority of Survey, the Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura of Makkah, ISNA, Karachi, Kemenag, JAKIM, Morocco, Jordan, Diyanet and the UOIF — and automatically choose the right default for each city. You can also switch between Standard and Hanafi Asr to match your school of thought.

Our calculation engine has been verified against the Aladhan API with a worst deviation of just 2 minutes across 17 city, date and method checks. Alongside prayer times, SalahTime offers a Qibla finder with a live compass, a Hijri calendar with Ramadan and Eid countdowns, and plain-language explanations of every method. Prayer times are calculated astronomically; please confirm with your local mosque.
